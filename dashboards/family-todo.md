# Family ToDo (admin pages)

Where jobs and dinners get *set up*. The wall panels only show and tick them; this dashboard is for adding and planning, from a phone or tablet.

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Jobs** | Add a job in three steps: *Who is it for?* (Dad / Mum / our son), *How often?* (one off, daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or high priority), *What needs doing?* (type and press return). The "Jobs - add when text entered" automation puts it on the right list. |
| **Dinners** | Plan this week's and next week's dinners, Monday to Sunday. These feed "Menu this week" on the Kitchen panel and "Tonight's dinner" in glance mode, and a weekly automation rolls next week into this week. |
| **Lists** | Everyone's live job lists, for ticking off or editing |
| **Schedules** | The repeating job templates per person (daily / weekly / fortnightly / monthly). The top-up automations copy these onto the live lists on schedule. Tick on the *Lists* tab, not here. |

Built from standard cards (markdown, grid, entities, to-do) plus a few button-cards for the big choice buttons.
